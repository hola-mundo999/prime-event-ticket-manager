About the club

Bridging the gap between academia and industry.

Prime IT Club is the student-run technology club at Prime College, Kathmandu. Through sessions, workshops, training programmes and competitions it connects students with the people actually building software in Nepal.

This portal is the club's own ticketing desk: pick an event, get a QR pass, and let the coordinators check you in at the door without a paper list.
